About me

I'm a project engineer with twelve years at ABB, where I started as a diploma trainee in 2014. I now run power management and substation automation projects from customer requirements through to commissioning and handover, for onshore and offshore customers including FPSOs, LNG and process plants across Asia, the Middle East, South America and Europe. I plan and schedule the work, track cost and quality, manage risk, and coordinate clients, contractors and engineering teams, often across several projects at once. My technical base is IEC 61850, ABB 800xA DCS and Power Management Systems, with OT cyber security practice under IEC 62443, and I train engineers and operators. I'm looking to move into a higher role in project engineering, product engineering or project management, where I can own delivery, develop teams and shape how complex systems are built.
